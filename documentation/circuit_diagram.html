1. Overcurrent & Emergency Cutoff
30A main fuse protects against motor stall shorts. The physical emergency stop mushroom button cuts main battery power to both buck converters and the H-bridge drivers instantly.
2. Dual Isolated Step-Down Regulators
Separate step-down converters prevent motor voltage transients and electromagnetic noise from corrupting sensitive camera vision streams and I2C IMU bus readings.
3. Star Ground Topology
All high-current motor grounds and low-power logic grounds converge at a single chassis star-ground junction to prevent ground loops and false sensor readings.
